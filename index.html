About Milena O Mileni

Emotional Precision.
International Range. Emocionalna preciznost.
Međunarodni doseg.

Milena Predić is a Belgrade-based actress born in Pančevo, Serbia. A graduate of the Faculty of Dramatic Arts in Belgrade, she has built a career spanning film, television, and theatre across more than fifteen years of professional work.

Milena Predić je glumica iz Beograda, rođena u Pančevu. Diplomirala je na Fakultetu dramskih umetnosti u Beogradu i izgradila karijeru u filmu, televiziji i pozorištu tokom više od petnaest godina profesionalnog rada.

Her international awards include Best Actress at the Oman Film Festival and the Chicago Serbian Film Festival for A Cross in the Desert (2022), and Best Supporting Actress at the Chicago Serbian Film Festival for Isolation (2024). She works fluently in Serbian, English, and Russian.

Njene međunarodne nagrade uključuju nagradu za najboljeg glumca na filmskim festivalima u Omanu i Čikagu za film A Cross in the Desert (2022), kao i nagradu za najboljeg sporednog glumca na Čikaškom srpskom filmskom festivalu za Isolation (2024).

Biography Biografija

Milena Predić is a Belgrade-based actress whose work moves between psychological realism and mythological intensity — a range that has brought her recognition on screens and stages across Europe and the Middle East.

Born in Pančevo, Serbia, she trained at the Faculty of Dramatic Arts in Belgrade, one of the most rigorous acting programmes in Southeast Europe. Her graduation period coincided with a breakthrough: her debut in Srđan Dragojević's landmark film St George Shoots the Dragon (2009) earned her the Best Debutant award at the Niš Film Festival.

In the years since, Milena has accumulated an extensive body of work across formats. In television, she has appeared in long-running Serbian dramas including Branilac (2021–2025), Time of Death, Underneath, Bad Blood, and The Sindjelics. In film, her most celebrated role is Saint Petka in A Cross in the Desert (2022), which brought her dual Best Actress awards at the Chicago Serbian Film Festival and the Oman Film Festival in 2023.

Her performance in the short film Submission received the Best Actress award at the Independent Shorts Award in 2019, and her supporting role in Isolation was honoured at the Chicago Serbian Film Festival in 2024.

Milena works in Serbian, English, and Russian, and holds accents in both Slavic and Russian registers. She is trained in stage combat, physical theatre, and improvisation, and sings as a mezzo-soprano. In 2025 she appears in five new productions, including the television series Robin Hood and the features Return of Zika's Dynasty, Sedef Magla, August, and How Come It's All Green Out Here.

Milena Predić je glumica iz Beograda čiji rad osciluje između psihološkog realizma i mitološke intenzivnosti — raspon koji joj je doneo prepoznatljivost na ekranima i scenama širom Evrope i Bliskog istoka.

Rođena u Pančevu, školovala se na Fakultetu dramskih umetnosti u Beogradu. Njen debi u kultnom filmu Srđana Dragojevića Sveti Georgije ubiva aždahu (2009) doneo joj je nagradu za Najboljeg debitanta na Filmskim susretima u Nišu.

Tokom narednih godina izgradila je obimnu karijeru u različitim formatima. Na televiziji se pojavila u dugotrajnim srpskim dramama uključujući Branioca (2021–2025), Vreme smrti, Ispod, Loša krv i Sinđeliće. Najcenjenija filmska uloga je Sveta Petka u Krst u pustinji (2022), za koju je dobila dve nagrade za Najbolju glumicu na Čikaškom srpskom filmskom festivalu i Filmskom festivalu u Omanu 2023.

Milena govori srpski, engleski i ruski, a poseduje akcentе na slovenskom i ruskom. Obučena je za scenske borbe, fizičko pozorište i improvizaciju. U 2025. godini pojavljuje se u pet novih produkcija.
