AboutO meni

Milena Predić is a Belgrade-based actress trained at the Faculty of Dramatic Arts. Over a career spanning more than fifteen years, she has built a reputation for emotionally precise, psychologically complex performances across film, television and stage. Her work has been recognised at international festivals including the Oman Film Festival and the Chicago Serbian Film Festival.

Milena Predić je beogradska glumica školovana na Fakultetu dramskih umetnosti. Tokom karijere koja traje više od petnaest godina, izgradila je reputaciju kroz emotivno precizne i psihološki složene nastupe u filmu, televiziji i na pozornici.

Short BiographyKratka Biografija

Milena Predić is a Serbian actress born in Pančevo, trained at the Faculty of Dramatic Arts in Belgrade. She works across film, television and stage, and has been recognised at international festivals for her depth of character and emotional precision.

Milena Predić je srpska glumica rođena u Pančevu, školovana na Fakultetu dramskih umetnosti u Beogradu. Radi u filmu, televiziji i pozorištu, a međunarodnim festivalima prepoznata je po dubini karaktera i emotivnoj preciznosti.

BiographyBiografija

Milena Predić graduated from the Faculty of Dramatic Arts in Belgrade, one of the most respected theatre and film training institutions in the region. Her stage work has encompassed classical and contemporary repertoire, developing a technical foundation that informs her screen work.

Her film debut in St George Shoots the Dragon (2009) earned her the Best Debutant award at the Niš Film Festival. Since then she has built a diverse filmography that spans lead and supporting roles in feature films, short films and long-running television series. Her work in A Cross in the Desert (2022) brought her international recognition, winning Best Actress at both the Chicago Serbian Film Festival and the Oman Film Festival in 2023.

In 2024, her performance in Isolation added a Best Supporting Actress award from Chicago Serbian FF to her credits. She continues to be active across multiple productions, with five projects in 2025 alone, demonstrating consistent professional demand.

She brings to each role a commitment to psychological truth, a trained physical and vocal technique, and a collaborative approach developed over more than fifteen years of professional work.

Milena Predić diplomirala je na Fakultetu dramskih umetnosti u Beogradu, jednoj od najuglednijih institucija za obrazovanje glumaca u regionu. Pozorišni rad obuhvata klasični i savremeni repertoar, razvijajući tehničku osnovu koja informiše njen ekranski rad.

Film debi u Svetom Đorđu koji ubija aždahu (2009) doneo joj je nagradu za najboljeg debitanta na Filmskim susretima u Nišu. Od tada je izgradila raznoliku filmografiju koja obuhvata glavne i sporedne uloge u igranim filmovima, kratkim filmovima i dugotrajnim televizijskim serijama.

Nastup u Krstu u pustinji (2022) doneo joj je međunarodno priznanje — nagradu za najbolju glumicu na Čikaškom srpskom filmskom festivalu i Oman film festivalu 2023. godine.
